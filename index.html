Reading your notes…

One exercise per line. Write it like your coach does: degrees (1-3-5 or do-mi-sol), "hold", "etc… to the octave", "down to 1", sounds ("with gee", "ee on root"), and a range (E2 - Eb5). Photos of typed or neatly printed notes read best. Messy handwriting may need fixing up.

    Cents off target (100 = a half step). Teal means within 25 cents, amber within 50, red beyond that.

    Lip-trill notes are scored on pitch only, since the buzz makes tone readings unreliable.

    Tone readings are estimates from the sound and vary with the vowel, mic and room. Use them to spot trends, not as a verdict.
