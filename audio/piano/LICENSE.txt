Piano samples in this folder

Source: Salamander Grand Piano V3 (a Yamaha C5), recorded and published by Alexander Holm.
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0), http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Changes made for this app: one note kept per minor third from C2 to C6 (17 files), downmixed to mono,
trimmed to 2.8 seconds with a 0.4 second fade-out, loudness matched, converted to 64 kbps MP3.
The exact steps are in tools/make-piano-samples.sh. The original recordings are not part of this repository.

Credit: Piano: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0, trimmed and converted to mono.
